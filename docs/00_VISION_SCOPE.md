# 00 — Product vision, creative pillars and release contract

## Identity
Working title: SIGNAL LOST. Private repo label: dreadfall-story-horror. Standalone Roblox sci-fi horror IP; references to 'Dreadfall' are repository provenance, not an instruction to share its existing PvPvE source or lore. Original Dreadfall remains untouched.

Logline: A maintenance crew enters Relay Nine to restore a quarantined communications facility; the signal learns to impersonate authority and needs the crew to complete its transmission.

Audience: friends who want a tense, accessible 25–35-minute shared story (solo to four), with more psychological tension than jump-scare spam. Horror imagery/maturity must be evaluated in Roblox's own questionnaire; design initially for mild fear without graphic violence. Do not target an age label before answering the actual questionnaire.

## Four player-facing pillars
1. **Together but uncertain**: shared danger, individual clues, clean team roles that scale down for solo.
2. **The signal lies**: emergency instructions gradually contradict environmental evidence, not player identity fraud or real voice cloning.
3. **Fear through anticipation**: good sound and light composition, telegraphed threat, occasional decisive scares.
4. **One complete arc**: satisfying reveal/ending in one session; not a broken demo or endless episodic tease.

## Promised player journey
Arrival -> restore power -> locate warning evidence -> calibrate three signal stations -> evade the manifested Echo -> sever/broadcast quarantine failsafe -> ending and optional debrief. Every player can understand the next task within 15 seconds after instructions; exploration remains optional.

## Scope ladder
**P0 PRIVATE ALPHA (7-day goal)**:
- One place, six to eight small connected spaces; greybox art acceptable if composition polished.
- 1–4 players on a server with stable synchronized progression; friend-led private testing, no production matchmaking promise.
- 25–35-minute first playthrough *goal*, achieved through narrative, two distinct puzzles, transitions, search and chase, not waiting.
- 3 acts, one final ending; 1 directed enemy/threat; one repeatable chase/avoidance.
- Keyboard/mouse, basic mobile touch, subtitles, volume and brightness options, player recovery and restart.
- Minimal on-screen task UI, progress and danger cues, optional clues; error-safe game completion.
- Server-driven instrumentation hooks and log tracing; no real Robux purchases in P0.

**P1 if gates pass**: seed variations (three curated layouts/events), optional 6 evidence fragments and journal, badges, a cosmetic earned free.
**P2 after live test**: transparent cosmetic passes, support tips, data persistence, creator dashboard funnels, fair pricing tests.
**P3**: two endings, additional chapter, complex enemy, dedicated matchmaking lobby/place, licensed original soundtrack and localization.

## Strict non-goals for the seven days
No open world, PvP, gun combat, heavy procedural map, voice-chat dependence, mandatory paid content, gacha/lootboxes, player trading, permanent perks that change outcomes, subscriptions, public advertising or sophisticated level generation.

## Quality bars
- 1 player and 2/4-player squads finish with zero blocking bugs over multiple real tests.
- Every puzzle works for solo; 4-player squad needn't wait for one specific designated player.
- Story has a clear premise, escalation, twist and ending. Long walks and filler do not count as content.
- First meaningful interaction within 60 seconds; major beats roughly every 3–5 minutes.
- No unreadable UI on phone, extreme flashing or uncaptioned critical instructions.
- Protected original repos. No unapproved payment flow and no claims of verified performance without measurements.

## Definition of 'best possible'
Prioritize stability, coherence, art direction, tension, polish and authentic tests over feature count. It's acceptable to reduce the final duration to a polished 18–25 minutes **with explicit owner signoff** if 30 minutes otherwise forces broken systems. Never silently claim a fully functional 30-minute co-op experience.
