# 36 — FINAL GENRE DIRECTION: CO-OP BLACKOUT SURVIVAL / EXTRACTION-LITE
**Owner clarification 17 November 2026:** The **realistic UK blackout emergency is the basis/setting**, NOT a restriction that forces a simulation genre. Choose the genre that maximises compelling gameplay, replayability, short-development feasibility and ethical monetisation. This is the current creative genre decision. Docs/27–35 remain applicable unless they contradict this decision. This is a design hypothesis, not verified success or a claim of likely virality.

## The choice
PUBLIC-FACING CATEGORY: **Co-op Survival**. HOOK: **Blackout Disaster**. FORMAT: **Round-based survival / extraction-lite thriller**. SECONDARY FLAVOUR: grounded social chaos, light suspense, resource-management. No claim to be a scientifically exact reconstruction; no full mil-sim, no endless open world, no mandatory monster-horror, no first-person shooter, no PvP criminals at launch.
**Creative ratio (qualitative intention):** 60% social co-op survival and preparation, 25% timed extract-to-safety or defend-home resolution, 15% authenticity and environmental storytelling. Not game-mode weights or mathematical telemetry.
**Core design mantra:** "Authentic world, dramatic situations, simple systems, different run."

## Why this format, not the alternatives (qualitative product reasoning)
- Pure realistic emergency simulator: distinct world and educational atmosphere, but first-time players risk early boredom, long exposition and repetitive errands. Realism can inform details without dictating pacing.
- Linear story horror: cinematic first run but expensive authored content and limited second-play appeal. Keep the opening blackout as strong story, but build repeatable decisions, not scripted monster/chase puzzles.
- Open-world roleplay/city sim: high content, AI and moderation cost; impossible to polish in seven active days; potential idle/chat without a useful core loop.
- PvP looter shooter / asymmetric traitor game: clips possible, but complex balance, mobile combat, griefing, realism problems, high exploit load and difficult QA. Defer player criminal roles.
- **Cooperative survival extraction-lite**: immediate comprehension, flexible squad size, one dense reusable British neighbourhood, authored news/event cards, short recoverable encounters, actual co-play reasons to retry. Extract means a viable SAFE outcome, **not** always physically leaving a home or looting people. No player-to-player theft.

## Short-form gameplay formula
1. **BLACKOUT (0–45 seconds):** unskippable cascade, immediate player control, working radio or wind-up fallback. First meaningful item in 60 sec.
2. **PREPARE (0:45–3 min):** simple three-item preparedness target, free torch/radio guaranteed; team can share carrying burden; safe indoor tasks have value.
3. **RISK RUN (3–9 min):** choose whether to check an open shop, optional ATM, help neighbour or stay and reinforce supplies. Random *curated* road/shop/looter changes; limited five-slot bag.
4. **COMPLICATION (9–14 min):** original fictional radio says high street unsafe or hub opened; one bounded looter encounter, reroute/assist/avoid; no forced combat.
5. **SAFE FINISH (14–20 min):** choose between PREPARED HOME (hold-out challenge and neighbour-help decision) or CONFIRMED ASSISTANCE HUB (safe path and limited access), maybe REST CENTRE only when specifically announced in future scenario. The game has an explicit satisfying end, not an endless survival bar.
6. **RESULTS (20–22 min):** show team members' outcome, preparedness, helped civilians, safe choices, optional clues, earned non-paywall cosmetic XP, replay as squad with one new crisis card.
Timing is a starting hypothesis; private alpha may be 12–18 minutes if that's what stable gameplay supports. No long artificial wait. Future beta 15–22 or up to 25 only if actual testers stay engaged.

## One sentence tutorial
"The power is out. Get what you need. Follow the radio. Find a safe way to make it through the night."

## One-round decision triangle
**SUPPLIES** (water/food/warmth/light), **INFORMATION** (radio, posted announcements) and **SAFETY** (stay, route, helper, evacuation only when directed). Players choose at least one meaningful tradeoff in each. A running shop is not guaranteed; cash optional, never paywall. Even if batteries fail, a public notice/hand-crank information fallback keeps all scenario paths possible. Loss of one nonessential item makes recovery interesting, not impossible.
Co-op roles EMERGENT only: runner, carrier, radio listener, local helper. No class-lock or mandatory four-person puzzle. Solo gets equal mandatory resource accessibility.

## Viral-worthy *moments*, not tricks
- Every streetlight dies sequentially while an original urgent radio bulletin starts; first player immediately has control.
- Friends reach a shop just as the cash machine stops working; one suggests the rear alley while someone hears danger on the radio.
- A looter steals a spare item, group decides whether recovery is worth risk; alternative available.
- Radio calls out that the hall is open in **this fiction**, while a dramatic street obstruction forces a route choice.
- Two friends vote to go to hall, another says "we are better prepared at home" and the group must decide; no sabotage or paid override.
Shoot real in-game footage later; no fake BBC government logo/alert outside clearly fictional entertainment context.

## Systems that make repeat play
Launch with **two validated scenario cards** when ready: CARD A "Stay Put", CARD B "Help Is Open" (one card sufficient for first P0). Each chooses from constrained, tested variant tables: shop open/closed, optional ATM working/offline, one road hazard, one looter patrol site, one item source, hall availability, and radio bulletin chain. NEVER randomise away all safe paths or all food/water sources. Each card has unique player choice plus viable path, not merely cosmetic RNG.
Results award **free** patches, radio journal pages, alternate headlines, preparedness ranking and friendly completion summaries. Persistent meta only if DataStore safe; no pay-to-win. Next cards: Night Two, temporary restoration, winter weather—with specific primary-source research, not manufactured official warnings.

## Development order shaped by genre
The initial vertical slice must be **fun as a 3-minute repeatable risk/reward loop**, not just scripted radio exposition.
P0: one home, shop, safe end, interact->take supplies->original bulletin->route decision->result->replay, 1/2 clients and real Studio restore. Looter and secondary route after loop works. Do not write long simulation economy code before a game can be won. Two solid complete rounds > enormous feature list.
Private-alpha target seven ACTUAL productive days remains aspirational; Day 3 must have full solo and 2-player round ending or cut optional cash, AI, second card. No guaranteed virality. User retention from actual testing decides genre-pacing tweaks, not genre-tag manipulation.

## Monetisation implication
Keep fully playable free and fair. Cosmetics for radio/tourch/backpack/fictional volunteer outfit, no extra bag slots, supplies or emergency advantages. Sell identity/expression, never security in a disaster. Free unlocks for repeats; potential group cosmetic collection after evidence of demand. Developer-product receipts only after explicit owner approval and secure verification. See docs/32.
