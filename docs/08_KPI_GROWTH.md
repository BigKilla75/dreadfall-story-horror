# 08 — User acquisition, analytics, marketing, and revenue measurement

## Marketing promise
A friend-group sci-fi horror experience lasting about 30 minutes, with a deceptive station broadcast and multiple optional secrets. Lead with a distinctive on-brand silhouette and signal waveform, not generic 'SCARIEST GAME EVER' claims. Truthful product naming is part of trust.

## Positioning
Roblox competitors to study: The Intruder for confined-space suspense, Short Creepy Stories for complete chapter packaging, Home Alone for simple tasks with dread, DOORS and Pressure for player expectations around group tension. These are references for craft, not assets/scripts to copy. SIGNAL LOST differentiates through cooperative facility maintenance + messages that contradict the evidence.

## Creative assets
- Icon: clean square composition, human silhouette against transmission tower/relay symbol, one focal point, legible at small sizes.
- Thumbnail 1: group of 3 at red-lit sealed communications door, no false gameplay screenshots.
- Thumbnail 2: first-person control console with waveform and shadow.
- Short teaser: 1 sec logo, 2–4 sec audible/visual signal glitch, one unexpected shadow, clear 'play with friends' CTA, actual footage once game exists.
- Title/description: mention 1–4 players, co-op, approximately 30 minutes only after measured, mild fear and no paid progress.
- Use original licensed music and images, Roblox community-safe promotional materials. Target images 1920x1080 for thumbnails.

## Launch sequencing
Phase A: closed testing 5–10 trusted testers. Record technical blocks and player confusion. No paid campaigns.
Phase B: private beta with 20–50 invited people, 1/2/4 groups; compare chapter completion and replay intent.
Phase C: public launch only after party, mobile, policy, stability and monetization readiness; post authentic clips to creator accounts and seek creator coverage without paid guarantees.
Phase D: weekly bug patch and modest scripted story detail/evidence additions, then next chapter only if retention justifies it.
Do not promise launch dates to marketing platforms until testing.

## Funnel metrics (instrument SERVER-side in published game)
install/visit -> joined run -> first interaction (<=60 sec target) -> act 1 complete -> act 2 complete -> chase resolved -> ending -> replay -> shop view -> legitimate purchase.
Track event-level errors; player count, device class and scenario seed in privacy-safe event attributes.
Internally proposed goals (NOT established Roblox benchmarks): >=90% first interaction within 60 sec; >=70% alpha testers reach Act 2; >=60% finish story on their first valid run; >=20% report interest in replay. These are learning targets, revise after test data.
Commercial metrics after sufficient traffic: DAU, average active session length, day-1/day-7 return, visits from friends/share links, qualified purchase rate, revenue/visitor, return on ad spend, customer complaints/refunds and earned Robux.
Segment solo vs 2/3/4, PC vs mobile, first run vs repeat, optional clues found.

## Creator Rewards
Eligibility has strict active-spender and timing conditions; no guaranteed payout from 30-minute sessions, and never generate fake play or alt engagement. Official program rules may change. Keep it supplementary to game sales, not core assumptions.

## Business/revenue limits
No one can forecast meaningful money without visits, payer conversion, traffic source, average price, creator share, churn and publishing/marketing costs. Record scenario assumptions in analytics before calculating revenue. Check DevEx current eligibility separately; 'sales' != cash.
