# 32 — COMMERCIAL PLAN / EARNINGS / ORGANIC GROWTH
Status: DESIGN PROPOSAL. No products activated, no sales, no advertising bought, no promised earnings or guaranteed virality.

## What is monetised
The **free full first-night story** (all supplies, maps, radio information, survival chances, destinations, difficulty settings and endings) is always accessible without Robux. Never sell food, water, money, radio batteries, shelter entry, hints, revive, spawn protection, looter immunity, extra inventory slots, faster team speed or "correct" emergency advice. No loot boxes, gacha, expiry FOMO or deceptive dark patterns, especially in a disaster-themed game played by minors.
Price candidates for later user testing, NOT created products:
- 49 Robux — Radio Housing Skins (cosmetic frequency dial colours/casing, same functionality).
- 79 Robux — Torch Finishes (cosmetic body appearance, identical light range/battery).
- 129 Robux — Brookmere Backpack Styles (visual only; no extra space).
- 199 Robux — Community Supporter Pack (original high-vis-inspired *fictional volunteer* outfit, nameplate + thanks, NO official emergency-service impersonation).
- Optional 25/75/150 Robux **tips** only after a safe repeatable developer-product purchase/ProcessReceipt implementation; never interrupt a threat/scene to solicit.
- Free earned badges/patches and original cosmetic rewards must exist so nonpayers feel respected.
No subscriptions or paid entry at first launch. Consider later updates funded by voluntary cosmetic purchases, not paid progression gates. Regional/managed pricing and platform fees/revenue share must be checked in Creator Hub before creating anything.

## Responsible timing
Alpha: monetisation locked (LivePurchasesEnabled false).
Closed beta: shop preview with clear "cosmetic only", no real spend until owner approves.
After reliable player experience: small accessible shop from debrief/main menu. No in-round purchase interruptions; no prompts targeting a failed player; no synthetic scarcity. Test purchase receipt and refunds/rejoins. If user doesn't buy, nothing gameplay-critical changes.

## Simple economics (estimates not promises)
Example: 1,000 daily active players x 3% unique daily payers x 80 Robux average gross spend per paying player = 2,400 Robux gross/day before Roblox fee, taxes, fees/currency conversion and DevEx eligibility; not a forecast. Even a 0.5% payer rate yields just 400 Robux gross/day at that spend. Do not equate gross Robux to GBP, or imply instant withdrawable income. Model user cohorts with real Roblox analytics, not fabricated benchmarks. Creator payouts/qualifications can change; recheck before projecting.
Never spend on ads before retention and first-run completion are healthy.

## Blow-up potential is a distribution hypothesis
Main hook: "The UK loses power tonight. Your mates have 20 minutes to get ready." Marketing must include "fictional Roblox scenario" and avoid claiming a genuine imminent blackout. One iconic image: dim British high street, red signal display, torchlights, figure at broken shop, "BLACK SKY" title. Original art only.
A strong 15-second clip:
0–2s: Lights cascade off; "THE UK HAS GONE DARK" plus fictional-game label.
2–5s: ATM displays unavailable; 2 friends grab supplies.
5–9s: radio warning about dangerous high street; camera turns to silhouettes at the corner shop.
9–12s: friends debate home vs assistance hub.
12–15s: "Would your squad survive Night One?" and clear Roblox CTA.
No fake BBC alerts, real news logos or fabricated official screenshots; avoid alarmist messaging outside clearly fictional context.

## Launch funnel and distribution
1. Product page: accurately named "OPERATION BLACK SKY [UK BLACKOUT SURVIVAL]" if title clearance passes; original square icon and two legitimate in-game thumbnails, measured descriptions. No "real government simulation" approval claim.
2. Onboarding: light-off in <10s, own role in <60s, no compulsory lengthy intro, invite a friend and non-voice co-op.
3. 5–10 private unbiased testers -> 20–50 opt-in closed beta -> instrumented small public soft launch *only after owner approves*. Reach UK survival/Roblox creators with transparent non-paid gameplay clips. Obtain permission for content reuse and disclose paid collaborations.
4. Launch updates: first polish patch within early observed problem window, and new scenario after stability; do not promise daily unrealistic live ops.
5. Community: simple vote on next scenario, limited to fictional assets; no unsolicited marketing/spam, no fake countdown to UK disaster.
6. Optional sponsored advertising only with budget/owner consent, after analytic evidence of retention; assess traffic quality by source. Roblox Home discovery uses real user play through, low bounce, repeat play, co-play and spend signals and is not guaranteed by buying ads.

## Metrics & experiments
Instrumentation when published: impression -> click/playthrough (Creator Analytics), joined -> torch -> first bulletin -> item obtained -> decision made -> scenario completed -> replay -> co-play -> cosmetic shop view/purchase.
Internal hypotheses, NOT platform benchmarks: >80% of testers pick first torch within 60s, >65% receive/understand first bulletin, >60% finish first scenario with one hint or fewer, <20% S1-related aborts, >25% voluntarily request another run in private tests, first 3-min bounce trending down each iteration. D1/D7 retention baselines only after actual cohorts exist.
A/B one variable at a time: tutorial task text vs icons, thumbnail 1 vs thumbnail 2, first looter timing. Do not falsify live data or report small-n private test as algorithm outcome. Publish only after clear data indicates the game is enjoyable.
